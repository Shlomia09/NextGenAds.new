## 1 · What the Owner allows

The Owner allows Rouge to use the campaign performance data from the accounts above (spend, impressions,
clicks, conversions, revenue, and similar metrics, at campaign, ad set and ad level) **only in aggregated,
anonymous form**, to build and improve the benchmarks Rouge shows its clients.

*Why it matters to you:* this is what lets your history help other beauty brands, without ever showing it.

## 2 · What Rouge will never do

- Show, sell or disclose the Owner's name, brand names, campaign names, collection names, creative, ad
  copy or account ids to anyone.
- Show any figure that belongs to the Owner alone. A benchmark appears only when at least 30 campaigns and
  90 campaign-days stand behind it (`rouge_benchmark()`, migration `20261118120000_reference_dataset`).
- Use the data to target or identify individual people. The data contains no information about the
  people who saw the ads.

Rouge stores the mapping between the Owner and its data in a table that no client can read, so the
provenance can be proven to an auditor or investor without being exposed in the product.

## 3 · Ownership

The Owner keeps ownership of its data. This is a licence, not a transfer: non-exclusive, royalty-free,
worldwide, for the purpose in section 1. Rouge owns the benchmarks, models and compilations it builds from
the aggregated data.

## 4 · Withdrawal

The Owner can withdraw this consent at any time by email to `[FILL IN: address]`. From then on, no new data
from the accounts is added, and the Owner's rows are removed from the reference dataset within `[30]` days.
Benchmarks already computed and published before withdrawal stay as they are, since the Owner's data cannot
be separated from them once aggregated.

*If you are worried about being locked in:* you can stop at any time, and nothing published can be traced
back to you.
